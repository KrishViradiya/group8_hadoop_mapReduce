Module Title: MapReduce System - Resource Management Layer

High-Level Descriptive Summary:
The Resource Management Layer within the MapReduce System is pivotal for orchestrating and allocating cluster resources efficiently. It encompasses components responsible for job scheduling, resource monitoring, and ensuring fair distribution of tasks across nodes. The layer's core role involves interfacing with the JobTracker, which is central to job scheduling and management. It receives job submissions, negotiates resource requirements with TaskTrackers (now replaced by NodeManagers in later versions), and manages job queues to prioritize and sequence tasks. Data flows through this layer as job proposals are submitted, evaluated, and assigned to available TaskTracker nodes. Dependencies include the JobTracker for centralized job management and TaskTrackers for execution. The purpose is to maximize cluster utilization while ensuring timely job completion. This layer's design inherently supports scalability by dynamically adjusting to cluster changes and fault tolerance through job redundancy and heartbeat mechanisms that detect and handle node failures.

The component layer consists of the following key elements:
* ResourceBundles: Simplifies internationalization property file handling.
* JobTracker: Central authority for job scheduling and management.
* TaskTracker (or NodeManager in newer versions): Executes assigned tasks on cluster nodes.
* JobQueue: Manages the sequence and prioritization of jobs.

Concluding Paragraph:
Performance optimizations in this layer are achieved through efficient queue management, intelligent task scheduling algorithms, and minimizing job setup times. Scalability is addressed by the layer's ability to handle increasing numbers of nodes and jobs without degrading performance, thanks to its distributed nature and dynamic resource allocation. Structural fault-tolerance is maintained via heartbeat messages that continuously monitor node health and enable rapid task reassignment in case of failures, ensuring continuous operation and data integrity.
