Module Title: MapReduce System - Job Management and Notification Layer

High-Level Descriptive Summary:
The Job Management and Notification Layer within the MapReduce System is pivotal for orchestrating job lifecycle events and ensuring timely communication of job status. This layer comprises components that manage job cleanup, facilitate job end notifications, and refresh job queues to maintain system responsiveness. The 'CleanupQueue' class is responsible for the background deletion of temporary files and directories generated during job execution, thereby optimizing storage usage. The 'JobEndNotifier' class acts as a centralized mechanism to notify various interested parties (like the JobTracker or user applications) about job completion, thus enabling timely response and subsequent actions. 'QueueRefresher' and 'CustomJobEndNotifier' components contribute to the dynamic refreshing of job queues and provide customizable job end notification capabilities, respectively. These elements work in concert to ensure efficient job execution tracking, resource management, and system-wide responsiveness, crucial for large-scale distributed processing in Hadoop environments.

The component layer consists of the following key elements:
* CleanupQueue: Manages and coordinates the deletion of temporary files and directories post-job execution.
* JobEndNotifier: Facilitates job end notifications to ensure all relevant components are informed of job completion.
* QueueRefresher: Refreshes job queues to maintain an up-to-date view of job status and resource allocation.
* CustomJobEndNotifier: Offers flexible job end notification mechanisms, catering to diverse user and system requirements.

Concluding Paragraph:
This Job Management and Notification Layer is engineered for scalability and fault tolerance. The background cleanup process managed by 'CleanupQueue' prevents resource bloat, enhancing system performance. 'JobEndNotifier' ensures that despite job failures or system partitions, timely notifications are disseminated, aiding in rapid recovery and resumption of operations. 'QueueRefresher' and 'CustomJobEndNotifier' components contribute to the layer's adaptability, allowing it to handle varying job loads and user-specific needs without compromising on responsiveness or reliability. Collectively, these components fortify the MapReduce system against potential bottlenecks and ensure consistent, efficient job processing even under heavy workloads or in the face of component failures.
