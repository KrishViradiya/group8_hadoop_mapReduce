Module Title: MapReduce System - Task Management and Reporting Layer

High-Level Descriptive Summary:
The Task Management and Reporting Layer within the MapReduce System is pivotal for tracking, coordinating, and reporting on the execution of individual tasks within MapReduce jobs. This layer encompasses classes responsible for task identification, context management, and reporting. The ID and TaskAttemptID classes ensure unique identification of tasks and attempts, while TaskTrackerInfo and TaskID provide information about tasks' locations and statuses. TaskReport and Reporter classes generate comprehensive reports on task execution, including successes, failures, and progress. TaskCompletionEvent and TaskFinishedEvent signal task completion, aiding in job completion tracking. The TaskAttemptContext and JvmContext classes offer execution context, crucial for managing JVM-related tasks. This layer's intricate web of interactions ensures that each task's lifecycle is meticulously monitored, reported, and managed, facilitating the overall reliability and efficiency of the MapReduce framework.

The component layer consists of the following key elements:
* TaskReport: Generates reports on task execution status and results.
* ID: Uniquely identifies tasks and jobs.
* TaskCompletionEvent: Signals task completion within the MapReduce framework.
* TaskTrackerInfo: Provides information about tasks running on TaskTracker nodes.
* TaskAttemptID: Uniquely identifies individual task attempts within a job.
* JobID: Uniquely identifies a MapReduce job.
* TaskAttemptContext: Supplies contextual information to task attempts during execution.
* MapTaskStatus: Encapsulates the status of map tasks within a MapReduce job.
* JvmTask: Executes JVM tasks within the MapReduce framework.
* Reporter: Generates progress reports for tasks and jobs.
* MapTaskCompletionEventsUpdate: Updates task completion events in the MapReduce framework.
* TaskFailedEvent: Signals task failures, aiding in error management.
* JobHistoryParser: Parses and interprets job history data for reporting and analysis.

Performance optimizations in this layer are achieved through efficient task tracking and reporting mechanisms, minimizing overhead and ensuring timely updates on job progress. Scalability is maintained by the distributed nature of task identification and reporting, allowing the system to handle large clusters without a single point of failure. Structural fault-tolerance is ensured by redundancy in task reporting and the use of unique identifiers, which help in reconstruction and recovery in case of node failures or job restarts.
