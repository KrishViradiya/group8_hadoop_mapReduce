Module Title: MapReduce System - Job Management and Cleanup

High-Level Descriptive Summary:
The Job Management and Cleanup subsystem within the MapReduce System is pivotal for orchestrating the lifecycle of MapReduce jobs, ensuring efficient resource utilization and timely job completion. It encompasses components responsible for job notification, queue management, and task cleanup. The CustomJobEndNotifier class provides a tailored mechanism for handling job completion events, extending the standard JobEndNotifier interface to accommodate specific application needs. The QueueRefresher component dynamically updates job queues, optimizing task scheduling and resource allocation. The CleanupQueue class manages the post-job cleanup process, releasing resources and ensuring no lingering tasks or data fragments impede subsequent job executions. This subsystem's core role is to maintain a responsive and scalable job processing environment, with dependencies on the MapReduce framework's core services for job submission, execution, and monitoring. Its purpose is to enhance the reliability and performance of the MapReduce system by ensuring timely job termination notifications and efficient resource reclamation.

The component layer consists of the following key elements:
* CustomJobEndNotifier: A custom implementation for enhanced job completion event handling.
* JobEndNotifier: A standard interface for job completion notifications in Hadoop MapReduce.
* QueueRefresher: A component for real-time queue updates, optimizing task scheduling.
* CleanupQueue: Responsible for managing and executing the cleanup of job resources post-completion.

Concluding Paragraph:
This Job Management and Cleanup subsystem is engineered for high performance and scalability, with fault-tolerance inherent in its design. By customizing job completion notifications and dynamically refreshing job queues, it mitigates resource contention and ensures that the MapReduce system can handle large volumes of jobs efficiently. The structured cleanup process minimizes the risk of resource leaks, contributing to the overall stability and reliability of the Hadoop MapReduce framework. These components collectively support the system's ability to scale with increasing job loads while maintaining consistent performance and fault tolerance through diligent resource management and job lifecycle orchestration.
