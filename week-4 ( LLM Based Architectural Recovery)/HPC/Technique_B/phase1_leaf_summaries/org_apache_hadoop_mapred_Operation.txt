Key Functionality: Submits a job to the MapReduce framework.
Core Logic: The core logic involves validating the user's access to the job and queue, and then submitting the job to the framework.
Inputs/Outputs: The input is the job configuration and the user's credentials. The output is a job ID that can be used to track the progress of the job.
Dependencies: The operation depends on the JobTracker class, which is responsible for managing the job queue and handling job-related operations.
