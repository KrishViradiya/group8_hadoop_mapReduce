Key Functionality: Manages shuffle header information for MapReduce tasks.
Core Logic: Handles the serialization and deserialization of shuffle headers.
Inputs/Outputs:
  - mapId: The unique identifier for the map task.
  - uncompressedLength: The length of the uncompressed data.
  - compressedLength: The length of the compressed data.
  - forReduce: The reduce task number.
Dependencies: None.
