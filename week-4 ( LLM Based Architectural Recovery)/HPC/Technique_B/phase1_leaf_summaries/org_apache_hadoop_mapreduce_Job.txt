Key Functionality: Submits a MapReduce job to the cluster.
Core Logic: Creates a JobContextImpl object and initializes it with necessary configuration and job information. It then creates a TaskAttemptListener object and starts the task attempt.
Inputs/Outputs: The job configuration, job ID, task attempt ID, and task attempt listener.
Dependencies: Configuration, JobContextImpl, TaskAttemptListener, FileSystem, Path, RawComparator, JobConf, ClientProtocol, ReservationId.
