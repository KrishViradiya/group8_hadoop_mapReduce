Key Functionality: Divides split information into two parts for different consumers.
Core Logic: The split information is divided into two parts based on the consumer of the information.
Inputs/Outputs: The first part is consumed by the JobTracker to create the tasks' locality data structures. The second part is used by the maps at runtime to know what to do!
Dependencies: org.apache.hadoop.mapreduce.InputSplit, org.apache.hadoop.io.Writable, org.apache.hadoop.classification.InterfaceAudience, org.apache.hadoop.classification.InterfaceStability
