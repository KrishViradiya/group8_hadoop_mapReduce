Key Functionality:
- The CheckpointService provides a simple API to store and retrieve the state of a task.

Core Logic:
- Checkpoints are atomic, single-writer, write-once, multiple-readers, ready-many type of objects.
- The CheckpointID for a checkpoint is released only upon commit of the checkpoint.
- Checkpoints cannot be re-opened for writes.

Inputs/Outputs:
- CheckpointWriteChannel and CheckpointReadChannel are provided for writing and reading checkpoints.
