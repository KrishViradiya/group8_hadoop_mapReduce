Key Functionality: Adaptor to run a C++ mapper.
Core Logic: Disabled the auto increment of the counter. For pipes, the number of processed records could be different (equal or less) than the number of records input.

Dependencies: JobConf, MapRunner, OutputCollector, RecordReader, Reporter, SkipBadRecords, MRJobConfig
