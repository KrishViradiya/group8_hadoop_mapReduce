Key Functionality:coordinates the commit of task output for a Map-Reduce job.
Core Logic:The Map-Reduce framework relies on the OutputCommitter of the job to:
- Setup the job during initialization.
- Cleanup the job after the job completion.
- Setup the task temporary output.
- Check whether a task needs a commit.
- Commit of the task output.
- Discard the task commit.
Inputs/Outputs:List data.
Dependencies:List classes.
