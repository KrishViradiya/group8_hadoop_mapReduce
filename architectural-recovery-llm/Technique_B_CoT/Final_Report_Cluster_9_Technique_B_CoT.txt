Module Title: Job Resource Scheduler Subsystem
High-Level Descriptive Summary:
The Job Resource Scheduler Subsystem is responsible for managing the allocation of resources for MapReduce jobs. It coordinates the allocation of resources for each job, including the assignment of task trackers, the distribution of job files, and the monitoring of job progress.

The subsystem is designed to be highly scalable and fault-tolerant, with a focus on ensuring that jobs are completed successfully and efficiently. It uses a combination of algorithms and data structures to optimize resource allocation and job scheduling, taking into account factors such as job priority, resource availability, and task dependencies.

The subsystem is implemented as a set of classes and interfaces that interact with each other to provide the necessary functionality. These classes include the JobScheduler, the TaskTracker, and the JobTracker, as well as various helper classes and interfaces for communication and coordination.

The Job Resource Scheduler Subsystem is a critical component of the MapReduce system, as it directly impacts the performance and efficiency of MapReduce jobs. By effectively managing the allocation of resources, it helps to ensure that jobs are completed quickly and with minimal waste, while also providing a high level of fault tolerance and reliability.
